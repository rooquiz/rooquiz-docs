# Scoring & Assessment Rules

"Scoring" means different things by quiz type: Quiz focuses on **right/wrong and totals**, while Scored Quiz focuses on **multi-dimensional analysis and reports**.

## Quiz: scoring and judgment

In a **Quiz** you can:

- **Set points**: Assign points to questions or correct options.
- **Judge right/wrong**: Mark correct answers; the system judges each question automatically.
- **Sum totals**: Points are added up automatically.
- **Passing score**: Set a pass threshold and show a pass/fail result.
- **Answer feedback**: Show respondents the correctness and explanation per question.

<Callout type="info">
  Points and right/wrong judgement are available in Quiz and Random Knowledge Quiz. Scored Quiz uses dimension formulas instead, and Outcome doesn't score at all.
</Callout>

## Outcome: winning by votes

An **Outcome** quiz doesn't score — it **counts votes**. Each option votes for one or more outcome types, and the type with the most votes wins; ties go to whichever comes first in the outcome list.

Every outcome can carry its own CTA button, wiring an entertaining result straight into a next step. See [The four quiz types](/en/getting-started/quiz-types#outcome-quiz).

## Scored Quiz: dimensional analysis and AI evaluation

In a **Scored Quiz**, scoring is more about "profiling" than right/wrong:

- **Multi-dimensional scoring**: Map questions to several dimensions; after submission, each dimension gets a score, often shown as a **radar chart**.
- **Result profile**: Produce personality/ability conclusions based on dimension scores.
- **AI evaluation**: Use AI to evaluate text answers for deeper insight.

### About AI evaluation quota

<Callout type="warning">
  AI evaluation consumes your team's **AI quota**. Quota is counted per cycle; once exceeded, AI evaluation is skipped for that cycle (basic scoring is still generated). See [AI Credits & Billing](/en/account/ai-billing) for billing, monthly resets, and top-ups.
</Callout>

## Levels and result-page actions

Split the total score into **levels** (0–60 needs work / 60–80 good / 80+ excellent). Each carries a name, colour and description, and can hold a **CTA button** — the low band might offer "Book a consultation". This is the step that wires an assessment into conversion.

<Callout type="info">
  Don't want respondents seeing a number? You can **hide the overall score** and keep only the level name, description and summary — better for quizzes that should feel like a diagnosis rather than an exam.
</Callout>

## Report generation

- **Synchronous**: Basic scoring is calculated immediately after submission.
- **Asynchronous**: Heavier computation such as dimensional analysis and AI evaluation is generated asynchronously; reports go through a "generating → completed" state, and the system retries automatically on failure.

For viewing, exporting, and delivering reports, see [Reports & AI Evaluation](/en/results/reports).
