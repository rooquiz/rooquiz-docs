# Form Settings

Everything about the quiz that isn't a question lives here, reached via **More Settings** in the top right of the editor.

## Quiz Information

Title, description, **Cover Image** and **Cover Color**. The title identifies the quiz in the dashboard and shows on the landing page; the description feeds [search indexing](/en/publish/promotion#search-engine-indexing), so don't leave it empty.

## Open/Close

A master switch between **Data Collecting** and **Not Collecting**. Once closed the link still resolves but no longer accepts submissions — gentler than deleting the link when a campaign ends.

## Submission Access

Answers "**Who can take this quiz?**" — three options, and the choice decides whether you get [leads](/en/leads) at all:

| Option | Meaning | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Open to everyone** | No sign-in; answer and see results immediately | Maximum reach, lowest friction |
| **Sign in to view results** | Anyone can answer, but must sign in with an email to see the report | **The main lead-capture option** |
| **Sign in to take the quiz** | Only signed-in respondents may answer | Invite-only or internal use |

<Callout type="info">
  Only the latter two produce leads reliably — an anonymous submission leaves no contact details. To spread widely *and* collect leads, choose **Sign in to view results**.
</Callout>

## The answering experience

- **Index Display** — None / Arabic Numbers (1, 2, 3) / Roman Numerals (I, II, III).
- **Time limit** — a countdown starts when a respondent enters the quiz; when it runs out they get a modal and their **current answers are submitted automatically**.
- **Answer sheet** — shows a question navigator in the sidebar so respondents can see progress and jump around. Worth enabling on long quizzes.
- **System text** — override the built-in button and prompt copy on the answer page.

## Language

Set the **primary language** and manage this quiz's **language versions**. Full details in [Multilingual versions](/en/editor/translations).

<Callout type="warning">
  Changing the primary language only re-labels the source content — it **translates nothing**. Add a translation to offer another language.
</Callout>

## Analytics

**Google Analytics** — enter a measurement ID and page views on the public quiz are reported to GA.

<Callout type="warning">
  Google Analytics is a **Pro plan feature**.
</Callout>

## Settings that live elsewhere

Share links, QR codes, embedding, social cards, source tracking, the booking section and the promotion kit are all under the quiz's **Share** tab — see [Publish & Share](/en/publish). Theme and personalized templates are in the editor — see [Appearance & personalized templates](/en/editor/design).
