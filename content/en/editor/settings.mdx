Everything about the quiz that isn't a question lives in the quiz's **Settings** tab (next to **Edit**, **Records** and **Insights**). It is split into seven pages, listed in the left menu.

## Access & visibility

### Quiz Open/Close

A master switch between **Open — collecting data** and **Closed — not collecting**. While the quiz is closed, **the quiz link and every report link already sent out show as unavailable**. Collected data is untouched — turn it back on and both work again.

### Submission Access

Answers "**Who can answer, and who can see the report?**" — three options, and the choice decides whether you get [leads](/en/leads) at all:

| Option | Meaning | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Open to everyone** | Anyone can answer without signing in and sees the report right after submitting | Maximum reach, lowest friction |
| **Email required to see the report** | Anyone can answer, but has to leave an email (or sign in) to see the report | **The main lead-capture option** |
| **Sign in to answer** | Only signed-in respondents can answer | Invite-only audiences |

With **Email required to see the report** you also choose whether to **Verify the email before showing the report**. It is **off by default**:

- **Off** — respondents type an email and a name and go straight to the report, which usually lifts completion. The address is unverified, so some will be typos or fakes.
- **On** — respondents enter a 6-digit code from their inbox. Every lead's address is verified, and they get an account on your team they can sign back into to see past reports.

<Callout type="info">
  Only the latter two produce leads reliably — an anonymous submission leaves no contact details. To spread widely *and* collect leads, choose **Email required to see the report**.
</Callout>

### Result sharing

Shows share buttons on the result page and attaches an auto-generated card to shared links. With it off, no card is generated.

### Search engine indexing

On by default, so the quiz goes into your sitemap and can be found through search and cited by AI assistants. Turn it off for drafts, tests and quizzes meant only for a specific audience. See [search indexing](/en/publish/promotion#search-engine-indexing).

## Basic info

**Quiz Title**, **Cover Image** (1200 × 630 px recommended) and **Cover Color**. The title identifies the quiz in the dashboard and shows on the landing page.

The quiz **description** is edited on the landing page in the editor, not here. It feeds [search indexing](/en/publish/promotion#search-engine-indexing), so don't leave it empty.

## Answering experience

- **Index Display Mode** — None / Arabic Numbers (1, 2, 3) / Roman Numerals (I, II, III).
- **Time limit** — a countdown starts when a respondent enters the quiz; when it runs out they get a modal and their **current answers are submitted automatically**. Available in **Knowledge Quiz and Random Knowledge Quiz only** — Scored Quiz and Outcome Quiz don't offer it.
- **Answer sheet** — shows a question navigator in the sidebar so respondents can see progress and jump to any question. Worth enabling on long quizzes.
- **Crowd compare** — turn on **Show choice percentages** and respondents see what share of people picked the same option right after they answer (e.g. "62% of people chose the same as you"). Only percentages are shown, never individual answers.
  - Off by default, and available in Scored Quiz and Outcome Quiz only — not in knowledge quizzes, where the most-picked option would give the answer away.
  - Nothing appears until the quiz has at least **10 submissions**; percentages then show up automatically.
  - Seeing what most people picked nudges later respondents toward the same option, so leave it off for quizzes that match courses or place learners.

## Result page follow-up

Two optional blocks for the result page. Their order on the page is set in the editor — see [Result page section order](/en/editor/scoring#result-page-section-order).

- **Booking section** — lets respondents book a call through your Cal.com page from their result. Turn it on, paste your **Cal.com booking link** (required), and optionally set the heading, description and button text — see [Bookings](/en/bookings).
- **Respondent feedback** — turn on **Show the feedback block** to ask **one question** on the result page. Choose the answer type: **1–5 rating**, **Single choice** or **Free text**. Answers land on the respondent's lead, so whoever follows up knows what this person actually wants.

## Language

Set the **Primary Language** and manage this quiz's **Language versions**. Full details in [Multilingual versions](/en/editor/translations).

<Callout type="warning">
  Changing the primary language only re-labels the source content — it **translates nothing**. Add a translation to offer another language.
</Callout>

## System copy

Override the built-in button and prompt copy on the answer page — navigation buttons, submission states, the time-limit prompts and so on.

## Analytics

**Google Analytics** — enter a measurement ID (e.g. `G-XXXXXXXXXX`) and page views on the public quiz are reported to GA.

<Callout type="warning">
  Google Analytics is a **Pro plan feature**.
</Callout>

## Settings that live elsewhere

The share link, QR code, share card, embed code and distribution plan are in the **Share** button at the top of the quiz — see [Publish & Share](/en/publish). Theme and personalized templates are in the editor — see [Appearance & personalized templates](/en/editor/design).
