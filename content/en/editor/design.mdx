# Appearance & Personalized Templates

Appearance has two layers: the **theme** controls colours and corner radii, while a **personalized template** controls the page layout itself. Most of the time the theme is all you need.

## Theme

Adjust it under **Theme Selection** in the editor; changes show live in the preview:

| Control | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Theme colours** | Primary, secondary, accent and neutral, plus info / success / warning / error |
| **Border radius** | Separate values for boxes, fields and selectors |
| **Effects** | Depth, noise and similar texture toggles |
| **Sizes & border** | Overall density and border weight |

<Callout type="info">
  If you have brand imagery, use **Pick colors from image** — upload one and the palette is extracted into your theme colours, which beats entering hex codes by hand.
</Callout>

## Personalized templates

A personalized template changes the **layout**, not just the palette. Two are available:

| Template | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Default** | The standard layout — clean and neutral, right for most quizzes |
| **Kinetic** | A design-led layout with a stronger visual treatment on the landing and result pages |

<Callout type="warning">
  Switching to Kinetic **requires Pro**. Note the gate is on the act of *switching* only: a quiz already using it keeps rendering, editing and duplicating normally on a free team — nothing is downgraded.
</Callout>

Switching templates warns that **your current personalization settings will be reset** — a different layout means a different set of layout parameters, and the old adjustments can't carry over. Duplicate the quiz first if you want to keep them.

## The landing page

The landing page is the first screen a respondent sees. In the editor you control:

- Whether the **title** and **description** show, and how they're aligned.
- The **cover image**.
- The start button's wording.

<Callout type="info">
  The landing page also affects [search indexing](/en/publish/promotion#search-engine-indexing): with it turned off, the quiz opens straight into questions and search engines have almost nothing to read. If you want to be found, keep the landing page on and write a real description.
</Callout>
