The visual editor is where quizzes get built. It has **three panes** — adding questions, previewing live, and configuring details all happen on one screen.

![The RooQuiz editor's three panes: question types, preview, and properties](/img/en/editor/editor-overview.webp)

## What each pane does

| Pane | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Left — question types** | Every question type by category; click or drag to add one. For a Random Knowledge Quiz this pane is the **Question Bank** tab instead |
| **Middle — preview** | A live render from the respondent's point of view. Switch between **Quiz** and **Report** at the top, and on the report side choose a **Preview Level** or **Preview outcome** to see how different results look |
| **Right — settings** | Detailed configuration for whatever is currently selected |

## The right pane's three tabs

| Tab | Contents | When it appears |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Properties** | The selected question's stem, options, required flag, score | Always |
| **Personalized** | Per-question appearance | Always |
| **Rules** | [Conditional logic](/en/editor/logic) | **Scored Quiz and Outcome only** |

<Callout type="info">
  If you can't find the **Rules** tab, it's usually not hidden — this quiz's type doesn't support it. See [The four quiz types](/en/getting-started/quiz-types).
</Callout>

## Common actions

- **Add a question** — pick a type from the left pane. Full list in [Question types](/en/editor/question-types).
- **Drag to reorder** — rearrange questions directly. Remember that [conditional logic](/en/editor/logic) needs the trigger question to sit before its target, so re-check your rules after reordering.
- **Duplicate a question** — copying and editing beats rebuilding a similar question from scratch.
- **Page breaks** — insert one to split a long quiz across pages instead of a single wall of questions.
- **Rich text and images** — question descriptions support rich text, including images.

## Question limit

On the **Free plan** a quiz can hold up to **50 questions**; **Pro** is unlimited.

- Only answerable questions count — page breaks, statements and image carousels don't. A random-question slot counts as the number of questions it draws.
- At the limit, adding or duplicating a question shows a notice and the upgrade prompt.
- **Creating from a template** or **duplicating a quiz** is never blocked, even past 50 questions. The new quiz follows the same rule as any other: you can edit and delete its questions, but not add more on Free.
- If your Pro plan ends, quizzes over 50 questions keep working and stay editable — you just can't add questions until you're back under the limit or upgrade.

## What's along the top

- **Preview** — open the full respondent-view preview.
- **More Settings** — go to [Form settings](/en/editor/settings).
- **Publish as Template** — push this quiz to the [Template Center](/en/library/templates) for your team to reuse.
