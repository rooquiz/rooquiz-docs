# Booking Availability

On the **1:1 Bookings** page, click the gear next to the title to open **Booking availability**. It has three sections: scheduling rules, meeting details, and approval.

<Callout type="warning">
  Only the **organization owner or an admin** can edit availability. Everyone else sees a read-only page.
</Callout>

![RooQuiz booking availability settings with scheduling rules, weekly hours and seats](/img/en/bookings/booking-availability.webp)

## Scheduling rules

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Timezone** | Every period is interpreted in this timezone. Respondents see times converted to **their own** |
| **Session length** | How long one session runs; availability is sliced into slots of this length |
| **Buffer between sessions** | Breathing room between meetings so they're not back to back |
| **Minimum notice** | The earliest someone may book, in hours — stops people booking 10 minutes out |
| **Book up to** | How many days ahead the calendar goes |

### Weekly hours

Add periods per weekday — "Monday 09:00–12:00", "Wednesday 14:00–18:00". A day can have several.

<Callout type="warning">
  Periods on the same day **must not overlap**; this is validated on save.
</Callout>

### Seats

Each period has its own **Seats** value — how many people can book one session in that period:

- `1` — one-on-one.
- `6` — a group of six.
- Empty — no limit.
- `0` — temporarily closed, but the configuration is kept for later.

<Callout type="warning">
  For **group sessions, set the buffer to 0**. Otherwise the first booking blocks the neighbouring slots and nobody else sees the remaining seats.
</Callout>

## Meeting details

- **Meeting link** — a fixed Zoom / Meet address sent out on confirmation. If you use a different link every time, **turn this off**: attendees then get only your instructions, or the link the lead owner adds when confirming.
- **Instructions for attendees** — how to join, what to prepare.

## Approval

Turn on **Confirm requests before sending meeting details** and incoming bookings arrive as **requests**:

1. The request lands in the "Waiting for you" section of **1:1 Bookings**.
2. The **lead owner** confirms or declines it.
3. Only after confirmation does the attendee receive the meeting address.

With the switch off, a booking is immediately final and the attendee gets the details straight away.

<Callout type="info">
  When in doubt, leave approval on. It filters out tyre-kickers and mistimed requests, and the only cost is one extra click from you.
</Callout>
