# Managing Bookings

The **1:1 Bookings** page is where you handle every session. Filters at the top narrow by quiz and status.

## Handling pending requests

With [approval](/en/bookings/availability#approval) on, new bookings land in "Waiting for you":

- **Confirm request** — confirms the meeting and releases the address. You can set a meeting link and instructions **for this one session**; leave them empty to fall back to the team-level settings.
- **Decline request** — you may add a reason, which goes into the email. They're notified and can request another time.

<Callout type="info">
  Reviewing is done by the **organization owner, an admin, or that lead's owner**. A request whose meeting time has already passed can only be declined, not confirmed.
</Callout>

## Actions on a confirmed session

| Action | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Reschedule** | Pick a new time from your available slots; the attendee is emailed and the 24h reminder is re-armed |
| **Mark completed** | It happened. Internal bookkeeping — the attendee is not contacted |
| **Mark no-show** | They didn't turn up. Also internal only |
| **Cancel booking** | Cancels and notifies the attendee by email |
| **View submission** | Jumps to the response they submitted |

<Callout type="warning">
  **Completed** and **No-show** can only be set once the meeting has **already started**. Rescheduling only applies to sessions in the Scheduled state.
</Callout>

## Adding a booking by hand

For sessions agreed over the phone or in a chat, record them directly:

<Steps>
### Click "Add booking"

Top right of the **1:1 Bookings** page.

### Pick the lead

Search by name or email. **Ordinary members can only add bookings for leads they own**; owners and admins have no such limit.

### Pick a time

**Available times** — choose from the normal slot grid, exactly what a respondent would see.

**Custom time** — enter any date and time, **bypassing the slot grid**. Useful for recording a special arrangement you already agreed.

### Decide about notifying

Tick "Send a confirmation email to the attendee" and they get a confirmation; leave it off and the booking is just a record on your side.
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
  A lead can have **only one active booking at a time**. Someone who already has one is flagged — cancel or reschedule the existing session first.
</Callout>

## How it ties into leads

Bookings and [leads](/en/leads) are wired together:

- The "Next up" block on a lead's detail page shows the upcoming session and lets you reschedule or cancel in place.
- The leads list has a **Next booking** column, and you can [filter](/en/leads/views#available-filters) by whether a lead has one.
- Every request, confirmation, decline, reschedule and cancellation is written to the lead's **activity**.
