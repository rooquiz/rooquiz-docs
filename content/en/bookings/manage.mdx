---

Bookings synced from [Cal.com](/en/bookings/calcom) are **read-only** in RooQuiz. To reschedule, cancel, confirm a request or mark a no-show, do it in Cal.com — the change syncs back here within moments.

## Where they show up

- **Lead detail** — the **Next up** block shows their next upcoming call with a countdown, the event type and the quiz it came from.
- **Submission detail** — a **Booking** section shows the call booked from that particular result.
- **Leads list** — the **Next booking** column shows each lead's next call, and you can [filter](/en/leads/views#available-filters) by whether a lead has one.
- **Lead activity** — booked, confirmed, declined, rescheduled and cancelled calls are all recorded.

<Callout type="info">
  Each synced booking carries a Cal.com badge as a reminder that it's managed there. The separate 1:1 Bookings page has been retired; bookings now live with the lead.
</Callout>

## Through MCP

An AI client connected over [MCP](/en/integrations/mcp) can list the team's bookings with `list_bookings`, filtered by status, quiz, respondent or time range.
