# 1:1 Bookings

The moment someone finishes a quiz and reads their result is the moment they're most willing to talk to you. **1:1 Bookings** lets them book a session with you right there on the result page — no third-party calendar tool, no email ping-pong about times.

<Callout type="info">
  Availability is a **team-level** resource: configure it once and every quiz shares it. You don't schedule your week again per quiz.
</Callout>

## Three steps to get it running

<Steps>
### Set your availability

On the **1:1 Bookings** page open **Booking availability** and set your timezone, session length and weekly hours. See [Booking availability](/en/bookings/availability).

### Turn on the booking section in a quiz

Go to that quiz's **Share → Booking** and enable **Show booking section on result page**. See [The respondent side](/en/bookings/respondent).

### Handle bookings on the bookings page

New bookings land on the **1:1 Bookings** page. See [Managing bookings](/en/bookings/manage).
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
  Until availability is configured, the result page shows **no** booking section even with the switch on — the settings page says so explicitly.
</Callout>

## The three sections of the list

The **1:1 Bookings** page is ordered by what needs your attention:

- **Waiting for you** — pending requests sit at the top when approval is on, because they're blocking someone else.
- **Upcoming** — confirmed sessions that haven't happened yet; the very next one gets a large card with a countdown.
- **Past** — history, collapsed by default.

The **1:1 Bookings** item in the sidebar carries a badge with the pending count.

## Statuses at a glance

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | A request awaiting confirmation by the lead owner (only when approval is on) |
| **Scheduled** | Confirmed; the attendee has the meeting details |
| **Completed** | The session happened — you marked it |
| **No-show** | They didn't turn up — you marked it |
| **Cancelled** | Called off by either side |
| **Declined** | You turned the request down |
