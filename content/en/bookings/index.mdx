The moment someone finishes a quiz and reads their result is the moment they're most willing to talk to you. The **booking section** puts your [Cal.com](https://cal.com) booking page right on the result page, so they can book a call without leaving — with their name and email already filled in.

RooQuiz doesn't run a calendar of its own. Your availability, event types, meeting links, reminders and reschedules all live in Cal.com; RooQuiz adds the entry point on the result page and, with the connector, brings each booking back to the lead.

## Two levels

| | What you get | Plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Booking section** | A Cal.com booking button on the result page, name and email prefilled | Free |
| **Cal.com connector** | Bookings sync back into RooQuiz: on the lead and the submission, in the lead's activity, in notifications and in your other connectors | Free |

Without the connector the booking section still works, but the booking only exists in Cal.com — RooQuiz doesn't know it happened.

## Getting it running

<Steps>
### Have a Cal.com event type

Create the event type respondents should book in Cal.com (for example a 30-minute intro call) and copy its link, such as `cal.com/your-name/intro-call`.

### Turn on the booking section in a quiz

Open the quiz's **Settings → Result page follow-up**, turn on the **Booking section** and paste your **Cal.com booking link**. See [The respondent side](/en/bookings/respondent).

### Connect Cal.com

Under **Connectors → Book calls**, connect your Cal.com account so bookings flow back. See [Connect Cal.com](/en/bookings/calcom).
</Steps>

## What syncs back

With the connector, every booking made **from a result page** is mirrored into RooQuiz:

- It appears on the [lead's detail page](/en/leads/detail) and on the submission it came from, and fills the lead list's **Next booking** column.
- Booked, rescheduled and cancelled calls are written to the lead's activity.
- The lead owner (falling back to the quiz owner, then the team owner) gets an **in-app notification** for each new booking.
- Booking created, cancelled and rescheduled can each [trigger a connector](/en/connectors/events), so you can post to Slack or push to Zapier when your calendar changes.

<Callout type="info">
  Bookings people make on your Cal.com page **outside** a quiz are ignored — only calls booked from a RooQuiz result page are synced, so your lead list isn't flooded with your whole calendar.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  RooQuiz sends **no booking emails**. Confirmations, reminders and reschedule notices come from Cal.com, to you and to the attendee.
</Callout>

## Statuses at a glance

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | The event type requires confirmation and you haven't confirmed it in Cal.com yet |
| **Scheduled** | The call stands |
| **Declined** | You rejected the request in Cal.com |
| **Cancelled** | Called off by either side |
| **No-show** | Marked as a no-show in Cal.com |
