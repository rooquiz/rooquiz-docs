Respondents see exactly one booking entry point: **the booking section on the result page**. This page covers how to turn it on and what the respondent experiences.

## Turning the booking section on

Open the quiz's **Settings → Result page follow-up** and turn on **Show booking section on result page**.

- **Cal.com booking link** — required. Paste the link of the Cal.com event type people should book, such as `cal.com/your-name/intro-call`; only `cal.com` and `cal.eu` links are accepted. With the [Cal.com connector](/en/bookings/calcom) you can pick an event type from a list instead.

Three pieces of copy are customizable; leave them empty to use the default wording in the quiz language:

- **Section title** — e.g. "Let's talk about your training plan".
- **Section description** — one line on what the session covers.
- **Button text** — e.g. "Book a free consultation".

In the editor's **Report** preview you can move the booking section up or down among the other result-page blocks.

## What the respondent does

1. Submits the quiz and reads their result.
2. Clicks the booking button. Your Cal.com booking page opens on top of the result page, with their **name and email already filled in** when RooQuiz knows them.
3. Picks a time and books it in Cal.com, which shows times in their own timezone and sends them the confirmation.

<Callout type="info">
  Booking needs **no sign-in** to RooQuiz. If the booking page can't open in place, it opens in a new tab instead.
</Callout>

## Matching the booking to a lead

With the connector on, the booking is tied to the submission it came from:

- If the submission already belongs to a respondent — they signed in, or claimed it — the booking goes on their lead.
- If it was anonymous, RooQuiz finds or creates a respondent and a lead from the **email entered in Cal.com**. The submission itself is **not** claimed by that email, because it wasn't verified.

To reschedule or cancel, the attendee uses the links in Cal.com's emails; the change syncs back to RooQuiz.
