The **Cal.com connector** is what lets RooQuiz see the calls people book from your result pages. It's a team-level connector under **Connectors → Book calls**, and each team can have **one**.

<Callout type="warning">
  The Cal.com connector is available on every plan, including Free, but only the **team owner or an admin** can set it up. The [booking section](/en/bookings/respondent) itself works without it.
</Callout>

## Connecting

<Steps>
### Open the Cal.com card

Go to **Connectors**, find **Cal.com** under **Book calls**, and click **Connect Cal.com**.

### Pick the data region

Choose **Global (cal.com)** or **EU (cal.eu)** to match where your Cal.com account lives.

### Authorize

- **Global** — click **Continue to Cal.com** and approve access. RooQuiz only reads your profile and event types, and adds a webhook so bookings flow back.
- **EU**, or if you'd rather not use OAuth — choose **Use an API key instead**. In Cal.com open **Settings → Developer → API keys**, create a key that never expires, and paste it in.
</Steps>

Once connected, RooQuiz registers the webhook in your Cal.com account automatically and pulls in your event types. Deleting the connector removes that webhook again.

## Picking an event type in a quiz

With the connector in place, the **Cal.com booking link** field in a quiz's booking section suggests your event types, so you can pick one instead of pasting a link. If you've just created a new event type in Cal.com, click **Refresh event types**.

<Callout type="info">
  Reschedules and cancellations happen in **Cal.com** — by you or by the attendee through Cal.com's own emails — and sync back into RooQuiz. There's nothing to manage on the RooQuiz side.
</Callout>
