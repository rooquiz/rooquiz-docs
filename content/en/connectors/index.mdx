**Connectors** push the leads and bookings your quizzes generate into the tools you already use — email, your own backend, Slack, or your email service provider. Find them under **Connectors** in the left sidebar.

<Callout type="info">
  Connectors are **team-level**: configure one and every quiz uses it. To let only certain quizzes fire a connector, narrow it with [trigger scope](/en/connectors/events#trigger-scope).
</Callout>

![The RooQuiz connectors page: connector cards grouped into Notify people, Send to systems and Sync audiences](/img/en/connectors/connectors-list.webp)

The page groups everything by what it does:

| Group | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Sending accounts** | **Gmail** — connect a Gmail account as a sending service with **Connect Gmail**. See [Email Sending Service](/en/connectors/email-smtp) |
| **Notify people** | Email, Slack |
| **Send to systems** | Webhook, Zapier |
| **Sync audiences** | Kit |
| **Book calls** | Cal.com — brings bookings made from result pages back into RooQuiz. See [Connect Cal.com](/en/bookings/calcom) |

## The five channels

| Channel | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Email** | Send a customizable notification to chosen recipients when an event fires — up to 3 per team |
| **Webhook** | POST to your own URL with an HMAC signature |
| **Slack** | Authorize the Slack app and post new leads to a channel |
| **Zapier** | Paste a Catch Hook URL and reach thousands of apps |
| **Kit** | Add new leads to Kit (ConvertKit) as subscribers and tag them |

Each connector fires on the events you pick — new submission, new lead, booking created, cancelled or rescheduled — see [Trigger events & scope](/en/connectors/events). Each connector runs independently — **disabling one does not affect the others**. Per-channel setup is covered in [The five channels](/en/connectors/channels).

## Permissions and plan

<Callout type="warning">
  Only the **team owner or an admin** can manage connectors. Other members get a no-permission notice, and the sidebar item isn't shown to them.
</Callout>

**Email**, **Slack** and **Cal.com** are available on every plan; **Webhook**, **Zapier** and **Kit** are **Pro only**. On a free team, selecting a Pro channel greys out the configuration area and offers an upgrade link.

## What the connector card tells you

Each configured connector shows:

- **Enabled / Disabled** — whether it's working.
- **Setup incomplete** — OAuth finished but a channel or final step is still missing. These do not deliver.
- **Last delivery** — when it last fired and how it went (success / failed).

<Callout type="info">
  After setting one up, hit **Send test** to confirm the other end really receives it, rather than finding out on your first real lead.
</Callout>
