# Exporting Data

Responses, leads and respondents can all be exported as **CSV** (UTF-8, opens directly in Excel).

<Callout type="warning">
  Export is a **Pro feature** with a weekly cap on the number of exports. Free teams see an upgrade prompt.
</Callout>

## Two kinds of export

| Method | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Export selected** | Tick some rows and export just those. Generated in your browser and **does not use your quota** |
| **Export all** | Exports everything matching your current filters. Generated on the server and **uses one export** |

<Callout type="info">
  Filter before you export. "Export all" exports the **current filter results**, not the whole table — the tighter the filter, the more useful the file and the less quota you burn.
</Callout>

## The confirmation dialog

"Export all" opens a confirmation that puts the important facts in front of you first:

- **Data range** — all data, or the current filter results (switchable right here).
- **Rows** — roughly how many. If the count fails you're told, and you can still proceed.
- **File format** — CSV (UTF-8, opens in Excel).
- **This week** — how many exports remain out of your limit, and when it resets.

Confirm and the file is **generated in the background and downloads automatically**; you can leave the page in the meantime.

## Quota and reset

The weekly allowance resets on **Monday UTC**. Both the dialog and the limit-reached message tell you the exact reset time.

<Callout type="info">
  **Failed exports don't count.** Only an export that actually produced a file uses your quota.
</Callout>

## When the dataset is too large

There's a row limit per export. Going over it is rejected outright (**without using quota**) and you get a ready-to-copy summary to email technical support, who will export it for you.

<Callout type="info">
  If an export seems to spin forever, the page stops waiting after five minutes and says so — the job is still running in the background, so come back to the page a little later.
</Callout>

## What's in the file

Beyond the answers themselves, exports typically include the record number, submitted-at time, respondent email and ID, and level. Lead and respondent exports have their own column sets.

<Callout type="warning">
  Exported files contain personal information respondents provided. Store and share them according to your organization's privacy and compliance rules.
</Callout>
