Insights aggregate scattered responses into **stats and charts**, helping you grasp the big picture without scrolling through records one by one.

Use the **7D / 30D / 90D** switch at the top to change the time window (30 days by default). **Export** downloads the current Insights view as **Excel (.xlsx)** or **CSV** — overview, audience, trend, funnel, channels, drop-off and answers. This is separate from [exporting data](/en/results/export) and doesn't use the weekly export quota.

## Key numbers

The top strip shows **Today's Submissions** (vs. yesterday), **Total Submissions**, **Unique Respondents** (signed-in respondents only), **Average Score**, **Report Completion** (completed / failed / pending reports) and **Shares** with the share rate.

## Common charts

- **Submissions in the last N days**: Response volume over time.
- **Answer Distribution**: For single-choice, dropdown, true/false and rating questions (up to 10), the share of each option.
- **Traffic Source**: Compare response volume across channels, combined with [UTM Source Tracking](/en/publish/tracking).
- **Login Method** and **Device Type**: Anonymous vs. registered respondents, and iOS / Android / desktop.

## Conversion funnel

The **Conversion Funnel** tracks **one visit (session)** through **Viewed → Started → Submitted → Report viewed → CTA clicked**. Two stages appear only when they apply: **Lead Captured** (after Submitted) on quizzes set to **Email required to see the report**, or that have lead data from before; and **Shared** (at the end) once results have been shared. How each stage is counted:

| Stage | Counted when |
| --- | --- |
| Viewed | The quiz page actually opens in a browser (crawlers and admin previews excluded) |
| Started | The respondent answers any question for the first time (not just clicking "start") |
| Submitted | The submission succeeds |
| Lead Captured | The submission is tied to a respondent — they left an email or signed in |
| Report viewed | The report finishes generating while the respondent is on the result page |
| CTA clicked | The respondent clicks the [CTA button](/en/results/reports#result-page-cta-button) at the bottom of the report |
| Shared | The respondent clicks a share button on the result page (first time, any channel) |

The percentage next to each stage is the conversion rate **relative to the previous stage**, so you can see exactly where the drop happens.

### Top 10 drop-off points

Among sessions that **didn't submit** but answered at least one question, grouped by the **last question they answered** — questions with high counts are where people give up: too hard, too sensitive, or the next one scared them off. Visitors who left without answering anything aren't listed here; they show up in the Viewed → Started decline.

### Conversion by source

Grouped by the link's `utm_source` (set by the [Distribution plan](/en/publish/promotion), [UTM Source Tracking](/en/publish/tracking) or the [Embed](/en/publish/embed) UTM attributes), comparing viewed/started/submitted counts and completion rate per source; sources coming from website embeds carry an **Embed** tag.

<Callout type="info">
  The funnel counts sessions since the feature launched; earlier historical submissions are not backfilled, so "total submissions" may initially exceed the funnel's submitted count. They converge over time.
</Callout>

## Suggestions

- Use the **conversion funnel** to locate where the drop happens: many views but few starts points to the landing page or the first question; many starts but few submissions — check the top drop-off questions.
- Use **Answer Distribution** to understand respondents' overall tendencies.
- Combine time trends with **conversion by source** to evaluate each channel's campaigns.

<Callout type="info">
  Insights show "group trends." To dig into a specific response, go to [Response Records](/en/results/records); for individual assessment conclusions, go to [Reports](/en/results/reports).
</Callout>
