# Response Records

Response records are **every single submission** a quiz received, under the quiz's **Records** tab. This is the raw data — for aggregate trends, go to [Insights](/en/results/insights).

![The RooQuiz response records list with the filter builder and table](/img/en/results/records-list.webp)

## Filters

Click **Filters** to open the builder and stack conditions (they're ANDed together):

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **By question** | Filter on the answer to a specific question; choice questions support "is any of / is none of / is empty / is not empty" |
| **Respondent** | Submissions from one person |
| **UTM source** | Which channel they came from, paired with [UTM tracking](/en/publish/tracking) |
| **Score** | A numeric range (**is between** a min and max) |
| **Level** | Which report level they landed in |
| **Language** | Which [language version](/en/editor/translations) they answered in |
| **Lead status / Assignee** | Filter by the [lead's](/en/leads) follow-up status or owner |
| **Submitted at** | A start and end date |

Hit **Apply** when the conditions are set, or **Clear all** to start over.

<Callout type="info">
  There's no board view here — the board lives on the [Leads](/en/leads/list-board) page. The two answer different questions: records are **submissions**, leads are **people**.
</Callout>

## A single record

Open any row and the detail splits in two:

- **Left** — the answers question by question with their scores, plus the report.
- **Right** — a follow-up sidebar for this person as a [lead](/en/leads): change the status, write **comments**, and read the merged activity timeline.

In other words you can do the follow-up while reading the response, without hopping to the leads page.

<Callout type="info">
  Comments support **replies**, and you can delete your own. They're the same comments as on the [lead detail page](/en/leads/detail#comments) — both places show one shared thread.
</Callout>

## What a record contains

Beyond the answers themselves, a record usually carries:

- **Record number** — an incrementing number within the quiz.
- **Respondent** — only present when they were signed in; blank for anonymous submissions.
- **Source** — recorded when they arrived through a [UTM link](/en/publish/tracking).
- **Submitted at**, **language version**, **score and level**.

## Exporting

**Export** on the toolbar handles either the rows you selected or everything matching the current filters. A full export uses your weekly quota — see [Exporting data](/en/results/export).

<Callout type="warning">
  Exported data may contain personal information respondents provided. Handle it according to your organization's privacy and compliance rules.
</Callout>
