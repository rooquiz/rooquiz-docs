# Responses & Analytics

After a quiz is published, all responses flow into the dashboard. Here you can view every response, export data, see visual analysis, and generate reports for assessments.

## How they differ

| Module | Focus | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Response Records** | Details of individual responses | Reviewing, filtering, and following up |
| **Insights** | Aggregate stats and charts | Completion rate, drop-off points, source conversion |
| **Reports** | Analysis of a single response | Scores, dimensional profiles, AI evaluation |
| **Exporting Data** | Getting the data out | Further work in Excel or your own tools |

<Callout type="info">
  Records for raw data, insights for overall trends, reports for individual conclusions.
</Callout>

## How this differs from Leads

This section is about **responses** — individual submissions. To work by **person** instead (how many times someone submitted, how far along they are, when you're meeting next), that's [Leads](/en/leads).

The two meet on the record detail page: open a response and the right side is that person's lead follow-up sidebar.
