Each response can generate a **report** presenting the respondent's score, level, dimension scores and profile conclusions — or, in an AI Quiz, a personal analysis written by AI.

## What's in a report

- **Dimension scores**: Scores per dimension, often shown as a **radar chart**.
- **Profile conclusions**: Personality/ability conclusions derived from dimension scores.
- **Overall score**: Drawn as a scale, ring, gauge or steps — see [Score style](/en/editor/scoring#levels-and-result-page-actions).
- **AI report**: In an AI Quiz, the report is written by AI from your instructions and the respondent's answers — see [AI Reports](/en/editor/ai-report).

### Hiding scores

Turn on **Hide scores** under **Settings → Answering experience** and respondents never see a score: the overall and dimension scores on the result page, each question's points while answering, the score in instant feedback and the per-question scores in the answer review are all hidden. Levels, analysis and right / wrong still show. Admins still see the scores in the dashboard. If the summary template inserts the score variable, the score still shows there — remove it to hide scores completely.

### Section order

In the editor's report preview, move result-page sections such as **Dimension Analysis**, the **Booking section** and **Respondent feedback** up or down to set the order respondents see them in.

### AI Match grading

In a **Knowledge Quiz**, a fill-in-the-blank question can be graded with **AI Match**: AI judges whether the answer is correct by semantic similarity instead of an exact text match. It is unlimited and uses no AI points (AI points are only spent on [AI Quiz reports](/en/editor/ai-report#ai-points-and-file-uploads)). In a Scored Quiz, fill-in-the-blank answers are collected but not scored.

## How a report is generated

- **Basic scoring** is calculated **immediately** after submission.
- **Heavier computation** such as dimensional analysis, AI Match grading and AI Quiz reports is generated **asynchronously**; the report goes through a "generating → completed" state.
- In an AI Quiz that requires an email to see the report, an anonymous respondent's report is generated only **after they pass the gate**.
- If generation fails, the system **retries automatically** within a certain window.

<Callout type="info">
  If a report stays in "generating" for a long time, refresh later; if the problem persists, try regenerating or contact support.
</Callout>

### Regenerating a report

Open a response in [Response Records](/en/results/records) and click **Regenerate report** to rebuild it with the current result settings — for example after you change scoring rules. The activity timeline records who regenerated it.

## Viewing and delivery

- **On-screen**: View directly in the dashboard; you can also let respondents view their own report via a share link.
- **PDF download (Pro)**: Turn on **Let respondents download the full report (PDF)** so respondents can save their report as a PDF. It only applies to quizzes that require respondents to sign in or leave their details to see the report.
- **Email delivery**: Send the report to respondents by email.

## On the result page

- **Respondent feedback**: under **Settings → Result page follow-up**, turn on **Show the feedback block** to ask one question on the result page (1–5 rating, single choice or free text). Answers land on the [lead](/en/leads), so whoever follows up knows what this person wants.
- **Crowd compare**: under **Settings → Answering experience**, turn on **Show choice percentages** so respondents see what share of people picked the same option, e.g. "62% of people chose the same as you". It's off by default, shows only percentages, waits until the quiz has at least 10 submissions, and isn't available for Knowledge Quizzes.

## Result page CTA button

A call-to-action (CTA) button turns a result into a next step — "Needs improvement" leads to booking a consultation, while "Excellent" leads to downloading advanced material.

- In the editor's **Report** section, switch on **CTA Button** and set a **default button** (button text and link; the lead-in text is optional). Text and link go together — fill in both or leave both empty.
- Give a **level**, an **outcome** or an AI **result tag** its own button inside that item. Anything left empty uses the default button; if the default is empty too, that result shows no button.
- Switch **CTA Button** off and no result shows a button, even ones with their own.
- CTA links always open in a new window.
- CTA edits **take effect immediately on all historical reports** — swap campaign links or copy without worrying about stale buttons in old reports.
- Respondents' CTA clicks feed into the conversion funnel in [Insights](/en/results/insights).

Want to skip the result page and send people straight to a page instead? Use [Redirect after submission](/en/editor/scoring#redirect-after-submission-pro) (Pro).

<Callout type="info">
  Links must start with `http(s)://`. The button style automatically follows your quiz theme color.
</Callout>
