A **lead** is the layer that turns quiz results into business. Everyone who leaves their contact details with your team gets one row on the **Leads** page, carrying a follow-up status, an owner, colour tags, a response count, and their next booking.

<Callout type="info">
  Leads are **cross-quiz**. If the same person takes three of your quizzes, there is still only one lead — with a response count of 3. Deduplication is by respondent, not by submission.
</Callout>

## Where leads come from

A lead is created automatically when **a respondent leaves their email** with your team — by signing in, or by typing their email to see the report — and their submission is attached to them. Anonymous submissions with no email create no lead — there are no contact details to keep.

So if you want a steady stream of leads, ask for an email at the moment people want their result. Two common ways:

- In the quiz's **Settings → Access & visibility**, choose **Email required to see the report**: anyone can answer, but has to leave an email to see the report (respondent journey: answer → leave an email (or sign in) → see the report). Each such submission produces a lead.
- Turn on the [booking section](/en/bookings/respondent) on the result page and [connect Cal.com](/en/bookings/calcom). Someone who books a call becomes a lead from the email they enter in Cal.com.

<Callout type="info">
  **Verify the email before showing the report** is off by default, so respondents just type an email to see their report. Those leads are shown as **Lead only** until the email is verified, and some addresses will be typos or fakes. Turn verification on if lead quality matters more than completion rate. See [Lead detail](/en/leads/detail#whats-on-the-page).
</Callout>

You can also **add a lead by hand** on the **Leads** page — useful for people you met offline or who came from another channel. See [Lead detail](/en/leads/detail#add-a-lead-manually).

## Leads vs. respondents

These two are easy to confuse:

| | Respondent | Lead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | A person's **account profile**: name, email, avatar, extended fields | That person's **follow-up record**: status, owner, tags, comments |
| Created when | They submit, sign up, or you create them manually | They leave their email with a submission (signing in or email-gated report), or you add the lead by hand |
| Where to find it | [Respondents](/en/respondents) | Leads |
| Can exist without the other | Yes — a respondent can have no lead | No — a lead always belongs to a respondent |

In short: **a respondent is the person; a lead is where that person sits in your pipeline.**
