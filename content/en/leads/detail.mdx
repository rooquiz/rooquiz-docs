# Lead Detail

Click any lead in the list or board to open its **detail page** — everything about one person in one place: who they are, which quizzes they took, what your team has said to them, and when you're meeting next.

## What's on the page

| Block | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Respondent** | Name, email, avatar, and whether they are "Registered" or "Lead only". Top right jumps to their full profile in [Respondents](/en/respondents) |
| **Follow-up** | Where you change status, assignee and tags |
| **Next up** | Their next upcoming [booking](/en/bookings) with a countdown; reschedule or cancel from here |
| **Responses** | Every submission this person has made; click through to a single report |
| **Activity** | An automatically recorded timeline of changes |
| **Comments** | Your team's internal follow-up notes |

<Callout type="info">
  "Registered" means they set a password and verified their email, so they can sign in to the [respondent portal](/en/respondents/portal). "Lead only" means they just left contact details.
</Callout>

## Activity

The activity timeline is **written by the system** and cannot be edited by hand. It answers "what has happened to this lead":

- Submitted a response, claimed an anonymous response, regenerated a report
- Who moved the status, and from which value to which
- Assigned or unassigned an owner
- Tags added or removed
- Booking requested, confirmed, declined, rescheduled, cancelled
- Lead added manually

## Comments

Comments are **internal** follow-up notes — respondents never see them. You can **reply** to a comment to keep a thread together, and delete comments you wrote.

<Callout type="info">
  Responses and activity show only the most recent few by default, with a "Show more" toggle at the bottom. That's a display-level fold and does not affect exports.
</Callout>

## Add a lead manually

For people you met offline or who arrived through another channel, click **Add lead** on the **Leads** page:

<Steps>
### Choose the source

**Existing respondent** — search your respondents by name or email and pick one. People who are already leads are flagged and can't be added twice.

**New respondent** — enter a name and email to create the respondent profile and the lead together. If you've configured [extended fields](/en/respondents/fields), you can fill those in here too.

### Write a first note

The "First note" is saved as the lead's **first comment** — a good place to record where you met.

### Save

The lead appears in the list, and its activity records that it was added manually.
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
  Deduplication is by email: if that address is already a lead, you're told it exists and given a link to open it, rather than creating a second one.
</Callout>
