# Filters & Saved Views

Once you have a few hundred leads, the ones you actually work each day are a small slice. **Filters** carve out that slice; **saved views** mean you don't have to carve it again tomorrow.

## Available filters

Click **Filter** to open the filter builder and combine these fields:

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Search** | Fuzzy match on respondent name or email |
| **Created** | All time / Today / This week / This month / Custom range |
| **Status** | One or more follow-up statuses |
| **Tags** | Matches a lead carrying **any** of the selected tags |
| **Assignee** | Leads owned by a given member |
| **Booking** | Has an upcoming booking / has none |

<Callout type="info">
  "This week" starts on **Monday**, and Today / This week / This month are all judged in **your browser's timezone**, not the team timezone.
</Callout>

Active filters appear as small chips at the top of the page; click the × on a chip to drop that one condition.

## Save as a view

Store a set of filters you use often and jump back to it in one click:

<Steps>
### Set the filters first

Pick the statuses, tags, assignee and so on, and confirm the list shows the people you meant.

### Click "New view"

Give it a name you'll recognise at a glance — "Hot leads to follow up this week". Names are limited to 50 characters.

### Switch from the top of the page

Saved views sit alongside **All** and **My leads** at the top of the page. One click switches.
</Steps>

Two views are built in, so you don't have to create them:

- **All** — no filters at all.
- **My leads** — leads assigned to you.

<Callout type="info">
  Views are **private** — only you see yours, and they never affect colleagues. A view stores filters, not a snapshot of leads, so its contents change as your data changes.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  Deleting a view removes only the saved filters — **no lead is affected**. There is a cap on how many views you can save; you'll be told when you hit it.
</Callout>
