## One page for signing up and signing in

There is no separate register page: signing up and signing in happen on the same **Sign in or Sign up** page. Use your email, or **Continue with Google**.

<Steps>
### Choose how to continue

Open the dashboard site. Click **Continue with Google** to use your Google account, or enter your email and choose **Continue with email**.

### Enter your password

If the email already has an account, enter its password to sign in. If it's new, the page switches to **Create Account** — set a password to register. When the page can't tell, it asks for a password and offers **New here? Create an account** below.

### Verify your email

A new email account gets a verification email. Click the link in it to verify your address. Full functionality is available only after verification.
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  If you were invited to a team, click the link in the invitation email directly. The system will guide you through signing up or signing in and automatically add you to that team.
</Callout>

## Reset your password

If you forget your password, click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page, enter your registered email, and follow the reset link the system sends to set a new password.

## Accounts and teams

- One account can belong to multiple **teams (organizations)**, and data is isolated between teams.
- Your role in each team (owner / admin / member / viewer) determines what you can do — see [Teams & Collaboration](/en/teams).
- Switch teams with the **team switcher** in the top left of the dashboard.
- Invite and manage members on the **Member List** page, under **Team** in the settings menu.

<Callout type="warning">
  Use a real, reachable email address: it is used for verification, password recovery, notifications, and team invitations.
</Callout>
