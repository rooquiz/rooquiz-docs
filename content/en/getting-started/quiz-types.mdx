The dashboard's **What do you want to make?** cards let you choose by goal. Behind each card is one of four quiz types (scenes), and the type decides how the result is worked out: right answers, option points, votes, or a report written by AI.

**Level test** asks one more question — **Do the questions have right answers?** Grammar, vocabulary or maths questions marked right or wrong make a Knowledge Quiz; self-ratings like "I can hold a conversation in English", where each choice carries points, make a Scored Quiz.

<Callout type="warning">
  **The type is chosen at creation and cannot be changed afterward.** That's a data-layer constraint, not a missing UI control. Getting it wrong means rebuilding.
</Callout>

## Knowledge Quiz

For trivia challenges and level tests with right answers — scenarios that need **right/wrong judgment and scoring**.

- **Scoring**: Assign points to questions or correct options; totals are summed automatically.
- **Judgment & feedback**: Mark correct answers, show respondents their score and per-question explanations, and split the total into **levels** by score band (e.g. 0–60 / 60–80 / 80+), each with its own result text.
- **AI Match for fill-in-the-blank**: Besides exact and contains matching, a fill-in-the-blank answer can be graded by **AI Match**, which judges semantic similarity against your answer. AI grading is unlimited.
- **Challenge features**: Add a **Time limit**, and turn on **Instant feedback** so respondents see what they got right page by page. Both exist only in Knowledge Quiz — see [Quiz Settings](/en/editor/settings#answering-experience).

## Scored Quiz

For assessments (readiness, health check or maturity scores) and self-rated level tests — scenarios where each choice carries points and you want **multi-dimensional analysis** rather than simple right/wrong.

- **Multi-dimensional scoring**: Define several dimensions, each scored by **its own formula** over the question scores, and show the results — often as a **radar chart**.
- **Conditional logic**: Show or hide later questions based on previous answers, enabling adaptive surveys.
- **Open-ended context**: Fill-in-the-blank questions only collect text — they are never scored — which suits a closing "anything else we should know?".
- **Report delivery**: Results can be shown on screen, exported to PDF, or sent by email.

## Outcome Quiz

For personality quizzes ("which X are you") and product or course finders — scenarios that produce a **result type** instead of a score.

- **Result types**: Define a set of outcome types up front (e.g. Lion / Owl); each type has its own name, theme color, image, and rich-text result content.
- **Choice voting**: Every choice votes for one or more types; after submission the type with the **most votes wins** (ties resolved by list order).
- **Result-page CTAs**: Each type can carry its own CTA button (book a consultation, view a plan…), turning a fun result into action and shares.
- **Question types**: Voting questions are single choice, multiple choice, dropdown, and true/false, alongside display blocks like Rich Text, Image Carousel and Page Break.

## AI Quiz

For assessments, reviews and coaching — scenarios where each respondent should get a **personal written report** rather than a score or a type.

- **AI-written reports**: After submission, AI writes each respondent a personal report from their answers, following the instructions you give it.
- **Answers and files**: Besides regular questions, respondents can upload images, audio, short videos or documents for the AI to take into account.
- **No scoring**: Questions carry no points or correct answers — the report is the result.
- **Result tags**: Optionally let the AI tag each respondent (e.g. *Ready to buy* / *Nurture*) so you can filter leads and route them to your connectors. Respondents never see the tag.
- **AI credits**: Each report uses 1 AI credit from your team's monthly allowance (Free 5, Pro 100), with top-ups available.

Setup details are in [AI Reports](/en/editor/ai-report).

<Callout type="warning">
  Available settings differ by type (for example, right/wrong scoring exists only in Knowledge Quiz, formula-based dimensions exist only in Scored Quiz, conditional logic only in Scored Quiz and Outcome Quiz, and choice voting exists only in Outcome Quiz). Confirm the capabilities you need before choosing.
</Callout>
