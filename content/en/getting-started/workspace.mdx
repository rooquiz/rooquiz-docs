# Workspace: Groups, Favorites & Trash

Once you have more than a handful of quizzes, the dashboard needs organising. This page covers four small things that help you find them faster.

## Groups

Groups collect quizzes together — by client, by quarter, by purpose.

- Use **Create Group** in the dashboard sidebar, give it a name and pick an icon.
- Names are limited to 50 characters.
- A quiz belongs to one group at a time.

<Callout type="info">
  **Deleting a group does not delete the quizzes inside it** — they simply become ungrouped.
</Callout>

## Favorites

Star the quizzes you open constantly and filter to them from the **My Favorites** tab at the top of the dashboard. Favorites are **private** — they don't change what colleagues see.

## Global search

The search box at the top of the sidebar looks across types: quizzes, question banks, respondents and leads. Jump straight there instead of first working out which page a thing lives on.

## Trash

Deleted items go to the trash rather than vanishing. It has three tabs: **Quizzes / Leads / Respondents**.

Each item can be:

- **Restored** — put back where it was.
- **Permanently deleted** — wiped, with **no way back**.

Rows show **auto-deleted in N days**, switching to an "auto-deleting soon" warning as the deadline approaches.

<Callout type="warning">
  Permanent deletion reaches different distances, and each dialog spells it out:
  - **Quiz** — all of its submission records go too.
  - **Lead** — follow-up history and comments go, but **the respondent is unaffected**.
  - **Respondent** — their lead, submissions and bookings all go.
</Callout>

## Collapsing the sidebar

The sidebar collapses to a narrow strip, handing the horizontal space to your content — useful when editing quizzes on a small screen. The collapsed state is remembered for next time.
