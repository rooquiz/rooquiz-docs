After signing in you land on the **Dashboard**, which brings together all of your and your team's quizzes. It is the starting point for day-to-day management.

![The RooQuiz dashboard: sidebar navigation and quiz list](/img/en/getting-started/dashboard.webp)

## The main area

- **Create cards** — the three cards at the top map to [Create Knowledge Quiz / Create Scored Quiz / Create Outcome Quiz](/en/getting-started/create-quiz).
- **Group tabs** — **All**, **My Favorites**, and any groups you created, with **Create Group** alongside them. See [Workspace](/en/getting-started/workspace).
- **Quiz cards** — each shows a type badge, cover, title, creation date and response count, with edit, records and more actions along the bottom.

## The left sidebar

| Item | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **My Quizzes** | This page |
| **[Question Banks](/en/library/question-banks)** | Reusable pools of questions |
| **[Respondents](/en/respondents)** | Profiles of people who took your quizzes |
| **[Leads](/en/leads)** | The follow-up pipeline for people who left contact details |
| **[Connectors](/en/connectors)** | Team-level push channels, **visible to owners and admins only** |

The search box at the top looks across quizzes, question banks, respondents and leads; the bottom lets you **collapse the sidebar**.

<Callout type="info">
  **Leads** is an optional team **Module**. The team owner can turn it off under **Basic Information** in the team settings. Once off, it disappears from the sidebar and every related page for all members, and no new data is created. Existing data is kept and comes back when the module is turned on again.
</Callout>

## Get your first lead

New teams see a **Get your first lead** checklist on the dashboard: four steps from zero to your first submission.

1. **Create your first quiz** — start from a template; scoring, result pages and the lead wall come pre-wired.
2. **Publish it** — open your quiz and switch it live.
3. **Send the link out** — grab the link or embed snippet from **Share**; a bio link, your site or a community post all work.
4. **Get your first submission** — once someone completes your quiz, their answers and contact land in **Records**.

After the first submission it turns into **You're up and running!**. You can **Hide** the checklist at any time.

## Tabs inside a quiz

Open a quiz and you get four tabs:

| Tab | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **[Edit](/en/editor)** | Build questions, logic, scoring and appearance |
| **[Settings](/en/editor/settings)** | Access & visibility, basic info, answering experience, result page follow-up, language, system copy, analytics |
| **[Records](/en/results/records)** | Every submission |
| **[Insights](/en/results/insights)** | Completion rate, drop-off points, source conversion |

**[Share](/en/publish)** is a button next to the tabs rather than a tab of its own. It opens **Share this quiz**, with four sections: **Share Link** (including the QR code), **Share card**, **Embed** and **Distribution plan** (per-channel links and copy, plus **Add a channel** for your own sources).

<Callout type="info">
  Quizzes used to have a "Connect" tab of their own. That has moved up to team-level [Connectors](/en/connectors) — configure once and every quiz uses it, instead of repeating the setup per quiz.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  The dashboard shows quizzes for the **current team**. If you belong to several, switch with the team switcher in the top left and the list follows.
</Callout>
