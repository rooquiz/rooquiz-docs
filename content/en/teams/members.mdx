## Inviting members

Under **Settings → Members**, enter an email and pick a role to send an invitation. The recipient joins via the link in the invite email (valid for 7 days). Registered users join directly; others are guided to sign up first.

## The four roles

A team has four roles, from most to least privileged:

| Role | In one line |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | The team's ultimate owner, with full access. Each team has **exactly one** owner. |
| **Admin** | Runs the team day to day: forms, question banks, respondents, leads, members, team settings. |
| **Member** | Builds quizzes and works the leads they generate. |
| **Viewer** | Reads. Cannot change anything, anywhere. |

The line between owner and admin is deliberate: **an admin runs the team, an owner owns it.** Anything that changes who the team belongs to, or its relationship with us as a paying customer, stays with the owner.

## Who can manage whom

- **Owner** and **admin** can both manage everyone else — including each other. An admin can appoint another admin, demote one, disable one, or remove one.
- The **owner's** own row is untouchable. The owner changes only by [transferring ownership](#transferring-ownership), never by editing a role.
- **Nobody can change their own role**, not even to step down. Ask another owner or admin to do it. Leaving the team is always your own to do.
- **Member** and **viewer** cannot manage anyone.

<Callout type="warning">
  Because admins can demote each other, treat admin as a role you grant to people you'd trust with the team. If you want someone to see everything without touching it, that's **Viewer**.
</Callout>

## Transferring ownership

Only the owner can transfer ownership, from **Settings → Members**. The new owner must already be an active member of the team. After the transfer you become a regular member — the team always has exactly one owner.

An owner cannot leave the team directly; transfer ownership first.

<Callout type="info">
  Want someone to see results without touching the quiz? Invite them as a **Viewer**, then share the relevant quizzes with them.
</Callout>
