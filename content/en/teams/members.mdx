## Inviting members

Under **Settings → Member List**, enter an email and pick a role to send an invitation. The recipient joins via the link in the invite email (valid for 7 days). Registered users join directly; others are guided to sign up first.

A team can have up to **3 members on Free** and **10 on Pro**, counting pending invitations. Need more than 10? Ask about Enterprise at [support@rooquiz.com](mailto:support@rooquiz.com).

## The three roles

A team has three roles, from most to least privileged:

| Role | In one line |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | The team's ultimate owner, with full access. Each team has **exactly one** owner. |
| **Admin** | Runs the team day to day: everything a member does, plus members and team settings. |
| **Member** | Builds and edits all the team's quizzes and works its leads. |

Everyone in a team sees **every quiz and every response** in it — there is no per-quiz sharing. Roles only decide who can delete for good, manage people and change team-level settings.

Deleting follows one rule: **anything you can undo is open to every member; anything you can't is for owners and admins.** Moving a quiz, lead or respondent to the trash can be undone for 5 days, so any member can do it. Deleting permanently from the trash, or deleting a response (responses have no trash), cannot be undone.

The line between owner and admin is deliberate: **an admin runs the team, an owner owns it.** Anything that changes who the team belongs to, or its relationship with us as a paying customer, stays with the owner.

## Who can manage whom

- **Owner** and **admin** can both manage everyone else — including each other. An admin can appoint another admin, demote one, disable one, or remove one.
- The **owner's** own row is untouchable. The owner changes only by [transferring ownership](#transferring-ownership), never by editing a role.
- **Nobody can change their own role**, not even to step down. Ask another owner or admin to do it. Leaving the team is always your own to do.
- **Members** cannot manage anyone.

<Callout type="warning">
  Because admins can demote each other, treat admin as a role you grant to people you'd trust with the team.
</Callout>

## Transferring ownership

Only the owner can transfer team ownership, from **Settings → Member List**. The new owner must already be an active member of the team. After the transfer you become a regular member — the team always has exactly one owner.

An owner cannot leave the team directly; transfer ownership first.
