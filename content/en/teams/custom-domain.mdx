# Custom Domain

By default, public quiz links live on the platform domain. With a **custom domain**, respondents see your own domain instead — better for brand consistency and trust.

Find it under **Settings → Custom Domain**.

<Callout type="warning">
  Custom domain is a **Pro plan feature**.
</Callout>

## Setting it up

<Steps>
### Enter your domain

Fill in the domain you want to use, e.g. `quiz.yourbrand.com`. A subdomain is recommended over your apex domain.

### Add the DNS record

The **DNS Configuration Guide** on the page gives you one record with three columns — **Type / Name / Value**. Add it verbatim at your DNS provider.

### Wait for verification

Come back and click **Refresh status**. The status moves between **Verifying** and **Verified**, or shows **Verification Failed**.
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  DNS records take **a few minutes up to 48 hours** to propagate, depending on your provider. Not verifying immediately after setup is normal — refresh again later.
</Callout>

## Status values

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Verifying** | The record is submitted; waiting on DNS propagation and the check |
| **Verified** | The domain is live and public links use it |
| **Verification Failed** | No correct record found — re-check Type / Name / Value against the guide |
| **Suspended** | The Pro plan has expired |

<Callout type="warning">
  When Pro expires the domain becomes **Suspended**: there is a **7-day grace period**, after which the custom domain stops resolving. Renewing restores it — you don't need to redo the DNS setup.
</Callout>
