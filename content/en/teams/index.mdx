Each **team (organization)** is an isolated workspace — forms, responses, and question banks all belong to a team. One account can join multiple teams, and you can **switch teams** anytime from the dashboard sidebar.

## Common tasks

- **Invite members**: under **Settings → Member List**, invite people and assign their role.
- **Manage members**: enable / disable / remove members and change their roles.
- **Switch teams**: pick or create a team from the team switcher in the sidebar. You can **own up to 5 teams**; there's no limit on how many teams you can join as a member.

## Setting up your first team

When you first sign in, a **Set up your team** dialog asks for a **Team name** and **Team address** — both appear in every quiz link you share — and lets you turn the Leads module on or off. You can **Skip for now** and change all of it later in **Settings → Basic Information**.

## Member limits

| | Free | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Team members | Up to 3 | Up to 50 |

Pending invitations count toward the limit. See [Account & Billing](/en/account) for plans and pricing.
