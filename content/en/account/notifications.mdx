# Alert Settings

**Alert settings** control which emails you receive. Find them under **Settings → Alert settings**.

<Callout type="info">
  This is an **account-level** setting, not a team one: it applies to **every team you belong to** at once. You don't configure it per team.
</Callout>

## The three you can turn off

| Group | Toggle | Sent when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Leads** | New lead | Someone becomes a lead for the first time |
| **Leads** | Lead assigned to me | A lead is assigned to you |
| **Bookings** | Booking activity | New bookings, cancellations, reschedules, and the 24-hour reminder |

<Callout type="warning">
  **Account, security and billing emails are always sent and cannot be turned off.** Password resets, email changes and charge notifications are outside these switches.
</Callout>

## Email alerts vs. in-app notifications

This page governs **email only**. In-app notifications (the bell in the top right) are separate and unaffected — turn the emails off and the messages still appear in your notification centre.

The notification centre filters by type: new submission, report ready, lead assigned, member invited, role changed, announcements, ownership transferred, and more.

<Callout type="info">
  The "email me on every submission" toggle has been **retired**. At any real volume those emails bury your inbox and nobody reads them. Watch the notification centre instead, or use a [connector](/en/connectors) to push submissions somewhere you actually look — Slack, for instance.
</Callout>

## What about your teammates

Everyone's alert settings are their own. Turning yours off has no effect on colleagues, so there's no risk of the team missing something because you unsubscribed.
