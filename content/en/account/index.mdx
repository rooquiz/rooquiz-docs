Your **account** is personal — it's you, across every team you belong to. The **plan** belongs to a team: each team is on Free or Pro on its own.

## Account settings

Find them under **Settings → Account Settings**. Here you manage your profile (photo, name, language, timezone), your email address and your password.

### Sign-in methods

The **Sign-in Methods** section shows the two ways you can sign in: **Password** and **Google**.

- **Link Google** — enter your current password to confirm it's you, then pick the Google account to link. After that you can sign in with **Continue with Google**.
- If your account email is already a Google account, you don't need to link anything — just use **Continue with Google**.
- **Unlink** — enter your password to confirm. After unlinking you can no longer sign in with that Google account.

<Callout type="warning">
  If Google is your **only** way to sign in, you can't unlink it yet. **Set a password first**, then unlink — otherwise you'd lock yourself out.
</Callout>

### Deleting your account

**Delete Account** at the bottom of Account Settings permanently deletes your account. You're removed from every team and all your sessions end. To confirm, enter your password and type `DELETE`.

- Teams you own where **you are the only member** are deleted with your account, and any paid subscription on them is canceled immediately. The dialog lists them first.
- Teams you own that **still have other members** are not deleted. [Transfer ownership](/en/teams/members#transferring-ownership) to a teammate first, then come back.

<Callout type="warning">
  Deleting your account **cannot be undone**.
</Callout>

## Plans

Each team has its own plan. See the current plan, subscription status and what Pro includes under **Settings → Plan & Billing**. Only the team owner can upgrade, change the payment method or cancel — see [Members & Roles](/en/teams/members#billing).

| | Free | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | Free | **$20 / month**, or **$192 / year** (20% off) |
| Team members | Up to 3 | Up to 50 |

Free and Pro both include unlimited quizzes and unlimited responses. Pro adds things like a [custom domain](/en/teams/custom-domain), [data export](/en/results/export), integrations and more team seats.

### Free trial

New subscribers get a **7-day free trial** of Pro. A **payment method is required** to start it, but you aren't charged during the trial and can cancel anytime before it ends. Once the trial ends, the subscription starts on the billing cycle you picked.

### When a payment fails

| Situation | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| The first charge after a trial fails | Pro is **revoked immediately** and the team moves to Free |
| A renewal fails for a paying customer | Pro stays on for a **7-day grace period** while the payment is retried; if it still isn't paid, the team moves to Free |

Paying the outstanding amount, or subscribing again, restores Pro.

### Nonprofits

Registered nonprofits and public-interest organisations can apply for Pro free of charge. See the [RooQuiz Nonprofit Program](https://rooquiz.com/nonprofit).
