When you share a quiz link to platforms like Facebook, LinkedIn, X, or Slack, the platform automatically fetches the **social share card** (Open Graph info) to generate a preview. Customizing this card noticeably improves click-through.

## What you can customize

- **og:title**: The main heading shown on the card (recommended within 20 characters).
- **og:description**: A short note below the title (recommended within 60 characters).
- **og:image**: The card's image, recommended 1200 × 630 px.

Leave any field empty and it falls back to the quiz's **title**, **description** and **cover image** — those live in the quiz's Settings. If there's no cover either, an automatically generated brand card is used, so previews are never blank.

## How to set it

In the target quiz, open **Share** → **Share card**, fill in the title and description, upload a preview image, check the **Preview Effect**, and save. If you close the dialog with unsaved changes, you're asked before they're discarded.

## Tips

- Keep the title concise and compelling, conveying the quiz's topic and value.
- Add a call to action in the description (e.g. "Find out your … in 3 minutes").
- Make the preview image's aspect ratio and clarity suitable for major platforms.

## Result share images

When respondents share their own result, the link carries an automatically generated **personalized result card** (see [Result sharing](/en/publish/promotion#result-sharing)). That image carries a **Made with RooQuiz** watermark in the corner.

<Callout type="info">
  Some platforms **cache** the fetched card. If the preview doesn't change after an update, it's usually caching — try again later or refresh with the platform's debugging tool.
</Callout>
