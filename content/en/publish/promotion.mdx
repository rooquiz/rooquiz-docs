# Promotion Kit

Once the quiz is built, the slow part usually isn't technical — it's writing the copy to go with it. The **Promotion Kit**, under **Share → Promotion Kit**, hands you ready-to-paste material.

## What's in it

| Section | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Social posts** | Three angles: a curiosity hook, the result types, and a challenge to your audience |
| **Email to your list** | A **subject line** and an **email body** |
| **Bio / profile link** | One line for your social profile |

<Callout type="info">
  Copy is generated in **this quiz's language**, so there's nothing to translate. The three posts differ on purpose — the same quiz posted to different platforms shouldn't use the same sentence.
</Callout>

## Every link is tracked

Each piece of copy carries **its own UTM-tagged link**. Post the three variants to three platforms and, with no extra work, the source breakdown in [Insights](/en/results/insights) tells you which channel actually produced submissions.

To build finer-grained source parameters by hand, see [UTM source tracking](/en/publish/tracking).

## Result sharing

**Share → Link** has a **Result sharing** switch. With it on:

- Share buttons appear on the result page.
- Shared links carry an automatically generated **personalized result card**.

<Callout type="warning">
  Turning this off also **disables the public share summary** — people opening a shared link will not see a result summary.
</Callout>

## Search engine indexing

**Off by default.** Turn it on only for quizzes you genuinely want people to find through search. It then:

- Adds the quiz to your sitemap.
- Lets Google index it.
- Lists the questions on the landing page so there's real content to index.

Leave it off for drafts, tests, and quizzes meant for a specific audience.

<Callout type="warning">
  Two situations make it nearly pointless, and you'll be told about both:
  - **The quiz has no description** — search engines show nothing under the title, and a title-only page rarely gets indexed. Add one on the edit page.
  - **The landing page is off** — the quiz opens straight into questions, leaving search engines almost nothing to read. Turn the landing page back on in the editor.
</Callout>
