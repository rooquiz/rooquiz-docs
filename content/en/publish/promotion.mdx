Once the quiz is built, the slow part usually isn't technical — it's getting it in front of people. The **Distribution plan**, under **Share → Distribution plan**, gives you **one card per channel** so you can post, track and compare in one place.

## One card per channel

Each card carries everything that channel needs:

| Part | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Ready-to-send copy** | Text written for that channel (the email card has a **subject line** too). Copy it, or use the card's action to open the platform directly |
| **Tracked link** | A link with its own UTM tags, so this channel's traffic is counted separately |
| **Numbers** | **Views**, **Started** and **Completed** that came in through this channel's link |

Built-in channels: **Link in bio**, **Story / short video**, **LinkedIn post**, **X post**, **Facebook group**, **WhatsApp / DM** and **Email your list**. Each card also has a short tip on how to use that channel well.

<Callout type="info">
  Copy is generated in **this quiz's language**, ready to send to your audience. Some networks can't prefill text — the post is copied for you, so just paste it once the composer opens.
</Callout>

## Next step

At the top of the plan, a summary shows views and completions over the last 30 days, and a **Next step** suggestion is worked out from your numbers — for example, nobody has opened the links yet, people land but don't start, people start but don't finish, or one channel converts far better than the rest and is worth posting to again.

## Add a channel

Posting somewhere that isn't listed — a podcast, a partner newsletter, a paid ad? Click **Add a channel**, give it a name and fill in:

- **utm_source** (required) — the platform, e.g. `podcast`.
- **utm_medium** (optional) — the placement or ad type, e.g. `cpc`.
- **utm_campaign** (optional) — the campaign name, e.g. `spring_sale`.

The custom channel gets its own card, tracked link and numbers, like the built-in ones. Two channels can't share the same utm_source / utm_medium pair, otherwise their numbers would mix. See [UTM source tracking](/en/publish/tracking) for how the parameters are recorded.

<Callout type="info">
  The plan saves as you go — there's no save button. The same per-source numbers appear in the source breakdown in [Insights](/en/results/insights).
</Callout>

## Result sharing

The **Result sharing** switch lives in the quiz's **Settings → Access & visibility** (see [Quiz settings](/en/editor/settings#result-sharing)). With it on:

- Share buttons appear on the result page.
- Shared links carry an automatically generated **personalized result card**.

<Callout type="warning">
  With it off, no card is generated — and on a quiz that gates the report, a forwarded link lands straight on the gate with no result summary.
</Callout>

## Search engine indexing

Also under **Settings → Access & visibility**. **On by default.** New quizzes are discoverable through search and can be cited by AI assistants. While it's on, RooQuiz:

- Adds the quiz to your sitemap.
- Lets Google index it.
- Lists the questions on the landing page so there's real content to index.

Turn it off for drafts, tests, and quizzes meant only for a specific audience.

<Callout type="warning">
  Two situations make it nearly pointless, and you'll be told about both:
  - **The quiz has no description** — search engines show nothing under the title, and a title-only page rarely gets indexed. Add one on the edit page.
  - **The landing page is off** — the quiz opens straight into questions, leaving search engines almost nothing to read. Turn the landing page back on in the editor.
</Callout>
