# Respondents

A **respondent** is the account profile of someone who has taken your quizzes — name, email, avatar, any extended fields you defined, and their submission history.

<Callout type="info">
  Respondents and [leads](/en/leads) are two layers: **a respondent is the person, a lead is that person's follow-up record**. A respondent can exist without a lead (someone who answered anonymously and left), but a lead always belongs to a respondent.
</Callout>

![The RooQuiz respondents list with ID, name, email, source and status](/img/en/respondents/respondents-list.webp)

## What's in the list

| Column | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Respondent ID** | A generated business ID, copyable, for referring to someone externally |
| **Name / Email** | Click to open the detail panel |
| **Source** | How this person arrived — see below |
| **Status** | Active or Disabled |
| **Email Verified** | Whether they verified their address |
| **Created At** | When the profile was created |

If you've configured [extended fields](/en/respondents/fields), they appear as extra columns too.

## The three sources

**Source** is written when the profile is **created** and never changes afterwards. It answers "how did this person get here":

- **Self sign-up** — they signed in or registered on the answer page themselves.
- **From lead** — created as a side effect of you [adding a lead by hand](/en/leads/detail#add-a-lead-manually).
- **Added manually** — you created them directly with **Add Respondent**.

## Adding and disabling

- **Add Respondent** — enter a name and email; the password is optional. Useful for importing a roster first and letting people set their own password later.
- **Disable / Enable** — a disabled person cannot sign in to the [portal](/en/respondents/portal), but all their past submissions are kept. Good for former clients or refunds.

## Moving to trash

Deleting a respondent goes through the trash and can be **restored within 5 days**.

<Callout type="warning">
  Deleting a respondent is heavier than deleting a lead: their **lead, submissions and bookings are hidden too**. If you only want to clear the follow-up record and keep the responses, use [move to trash on the lead](/en/leads/list-board#bulk-actions) instead.
</Callout>

Bulk delete works the same way — select several rows and they all go to the trash.
