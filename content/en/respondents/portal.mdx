Respondents aren't only anonymous visitors who submit and leave — they can sign in, revisit their reports, and manage their own bookings. That self-service layer is also what makes [leads](/en/leads) possible in the first place.

## When sign-in is required

Answering a quiz requires signing in **only** when its [Submission Access](/en/editor/settings#submission-access) is set to **Sign in to answer**. Otherwise, two things trigger it:

- **Seeing the full report** — if the quiz makes the report visible only after sign-in, respondents must sign in to see their complete result.
- **Booking** — the [booking section](/en/bookings/respondent) on the result page requires a sign-in, so the attendee can reschedule or cancel on their own later.

Respondents sign in with an **email code**, **Google**, or a **password** if they have one — no prior registration needed. Signed-in respondents can set or change their password in the portal.

<Callout type="info">
  Signing in **claims** the anonymous submission: that response becomes theirs and the matching lead is created. With **Email required to see the report** and verification left off (the default), typing an email also creates a lead — but the address is unverified and **no account is created** automatically; the report page still invites them to sign up and save it.
</Callout>

## The portal

Once signed in, respondents get their own portal with three tabs:

| Tab | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Records** | Every response they've submitted to your team, with their reports |
| **Bookings** | The sessions they've booked, with self-service reschedule and cancel |
| **Account** | Name, avatar, **Change password**, email preferences, and any [extended fields](/en/respondents/fields) you configured |

Under **Emails from** followed by your team name, respondents choose **I'd like to receive emails** or **Don't email me**, which updates their marketing consent. Their email address can't be changed in the portal — your team can change it from [Respondents](/en/respondents#changing-a-respondents-email).

<Callout type="info">
  The portal is **scoped per team**: in team A a respondent sees only team A's records and bookings.
</Callout>

## How it maps back to the dashboard

What respondents do in the portal shows up in your dashboard right away:

- They reschedule or cancel → the [1:1 Bookings](/en/bookings/manage) list updates and the lead's activity records it.
- They fill in an extended field → the matching column in [Respondents](/en/respondents) updates.
- They sign in and claim a response → "claimed an anonymous response" appears in the lead's activity.

<Callout type="warning">
  **Disabling** a respondent in the dashboard stops them signing in to the portal, but keeps all their past submissions — you still see everything on your side.
</Callout>
