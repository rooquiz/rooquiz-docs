Templates are the reuse mechanism in RooQuiz: each one is a **complete quiz structure** — questions, scoring and report configuration — that you clone into a new quiz and then edit. They're good for running the same kind of assessment for different clients or audiences.

<Callout type="info">
  To reuse a quiz of your own, **duplicate** it from the quiz list instead — templates are curated by RooQuiz.
</Callout>

## Templates

The Template Center holds complete quizzes, curated by RooQuiz, that you can clone. Start fast from a template, then edit your copy freely.

See [Template Center](/en/library/templates).
