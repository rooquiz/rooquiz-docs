A question bank is a pool of **questions you can draw from repeatedly**. Find it under **Question Banks** in the left sidebar.

<Callout type="warning">
  Every question in a bank **must have a correct answer** — banks are scored question pools, not a store for data-collection fields.
</Callout>

![RooQuiz question banks list with question counts and difficulty](/img/en/library/question-banks.webp)

## Creating a bank

<Steps>
### Click "Create Question Bank"

The subtitle reads "Knowledge Quiz or Scored Quiz" — banks serve those two scored scenarios.

### Fill in the basics

**Bank Name** (up to 50 characters), **Description** (up to 200 characters), and **Difficulty Level**.

### Add questions

Open the bank and click **Add Question**. It works like authoring in the editor: pick a type, write the stem and options, set the correct answer.
</Steps>

## Limits

| | Free | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Question banks per team | Up to 3 | Up to 30 |
| Questions per bank | Up to 500 | Up to 500 |

## Copying a public question bank

RooQuiz also publishes free **public question banks** at [rooquiz.com/explore/question-banks](https://rooquiz.com/explore/question-banks). They are preview only — to use one, click **Copy to my question banks**. That makes your own editable copy in your team, which you can then add to, edit and draw from.

- Only the **team owner or an admin** can copy a question bank.
- If you already copied that bank, you'll see "You already have a copy of this question bank" instead of a second copy.
- Very large banks are truncated: "This bank is very large — only the first N questions were copied".

## Managing questions

- **Drag to reorder** — rearrange questions directly.
- **Batch Delete** — tick several and remove them at once. This **cannot be undone**.
- **Edit Info** — change the bank's name, description and difficulty.

<Callout type="warning">
  A bank **must be emptied before it can be deleted** — you'll see "Please delete all questions before deleting this question bank". That guard exists so you don't wipe a whole bank by accident.
</Callout>

## Using a bank in a quiz

On the dashboard, click **Create Knowledge Quiz** and switch to the **Create Random Knowledge Quiz** tab. Pick a question bank, then set the **Draw Rules**: for each question type, how many questions to draw and the score per question. Each respondent gets an independent random draw.

**Question Lock Duration** decides how long a respondent keeps the same set: while locked, refreshing or coming back shows the same questions; after it expires, a new set is drawn. Choose **No lock (re-draw on every refresh)** or a window from 30 minutes to 24 hours.

<Callout type="warning">
  What you get is a **Random Knowledge Quiz** — a different type from Quiz. **The type cannot be changed after creation**, so switching between fixed and random questions means rebuilding. See [The four quiz types](/en/getting-started/quiz-types).
</Callout>
